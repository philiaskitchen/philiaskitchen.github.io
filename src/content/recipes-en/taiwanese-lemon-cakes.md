---
title: Taiwanese Lemon Cakes
intro: Taiwanese lemon cakes are a childhood favourite for many people — this batch makes about 10–12. The cake is dense and soft like a sponge cake, covered in a crisp lemon chocolate coating.
prepTime: about 30 min
waitLabel: Bake
waitTime: 15–18 min
cookLabel: Chill to set
cookTime: about 10 min
totalTime: about 1 hr 15 min (incl. cooling)
servings: 10–12 cakes
ingredients:
  - group: Cake
    items:
      - name: Whole eggs (room temperature)
        amount: "3"
      - name: Caster sugar
        amount: 80 g
      - name: Cake flour (sifted)
        amount: 100 g
      - name: Unsalted butter (melted over hot water)
        amount: 30 g
      - name: Honey (for moisture and flavour)
        amount: 10 g
      - name: Lemon juice
        amount: 5 ml
  - group: Lemon coating
    items:
      - name: Lemon chocolate (or white chocolate with a little lemon zest and yellow food colouring)
        amount: 150 g
      - name: Coconut oil (for shine, optional)
        amount: 5–10 g
  - group: Tools
    items:
      - name: Lemon cake mould
        amount: ""
      - name: Electric mixer
        amount: ""
      - name: Heatproof bowl for melting chocolate
        amount: ""
steps:
  - title: Preheat and prepare the mould
    text: Preheat the oven to 170°C. Brush the mould with a thin layer of butter and dust it with a little flour so the cakes don't stick.
  - title: Whip the eggs
    text: Put the eggs, sugar and honey in a bowl and sit it over a bowl of warm water — warming the eggs to about 40°C makes them easier to whip. Whip on high speed with an electric mixer until the mixture turns pale and grows in volume, and the trail it leaves doesn't disappear straight away.
  - title: Fold in the flour
    text: Add the sifted cake flour in two batches and fold it in gently with a spatula, lifting from the bottom, so you don't knock the air out.
  - title: Add the butter
    text: Mix the melted butter with the lemon juice. Stir a little of the batter into the butter first, then pour it back into the main bowl and mix quickly.
  - title: Fill the mould and bake
    text: Put the batter in a piping bag and fill each mould about 80% full. Bake until golden on top and springy when you touch them.
    timer: Bake at 170°C 15–18 min
  - title: Unmould and cool
    text: Take the cakes out of the mould as soon as they come out of the oven, and let them cool completely on a rack before coating.
  - title: Melt the chocolate
    text: Chop the lemon chocolate and melt it over hot water (no hotter than 50°C) until smooth, then stir in the coconut oil.
  - title: Coat the cakes
    text: |-
      [Dip] Hold each cooled cake upside down, dip the top into the chocolate and lift it out quickly.
      [Back in the mould] Pour the chocolate into the clean mould, put the cakes back in, and unmould once the chocolate has set — the surface will be very smooth and shiny.
  - title: Chill to set
    text: Chill in the fridge until the chocolate is completely set.
    timer: Chill 10 min
tips:
  - The eggs whip most easily when they are about body temperature.
  - Mix quickly once the butter goes in — over-mixing makes the cake tough.
  - For more flavour in the coating, add a little freshly grated lemon zest to the chocolate.
