---
title: Lattice Apple Pie
intro: A classic American lattice apple pie. The woven top looks lovely and also lets steam escape, so the filling thickens as it bakes. Flaky homemade pastry around cinnamon apples — best with a scoop of ice cream.
prepTime: about 1 hour
waitLabel: Chill the pastry
waitTime: 1 hour or more
cookLabel: Bake
cookTime: 45–50 min
totalTime: about 4 hours (including cooling)
servings: 1 pie (8- or 9-inch pie dish)
ingredients:
  - group: Classic pie crust (pâte brisée)
    items:
      - name: Plain flour
        amount: 250 g
      - name: Unsalted butter (very cold, cut into 1 cm cubes)
        amount: 140 g
      - name: Caster sugar
        amount: 15 g
      - name: Salt
        amount: 3 g
      - name: Iced water (adjust to how much your flour absorbs)
        amount: about 50–70 ml
  - group: Cinnamon apple filling
    items:
      - name: Tart, firm apples (such as Granny Smith or Pink Lady)
        amount: 4–5
      - name: Unsalted butter
        amount: 15 g
      - name: Caster sugar or brown sugar (adjust to the sweetness of your apples)
        amount: 60 g
      - name: Lemon juice (stops browning and adds flavour)
        amount: 1 tbsp (about 15 ml)
      - name: Ground cinnamon (use less or leave out if you don't like it)
        amount: 1 tsp (about 3 g)
      - name: Cornflour (cornstarch, to thicken the juices)
        amount: 1.5 tbsp
  - group: Topping
    items:
      - name: Beaten egg (for brushing)
        amount: "1"
      - name: Raw sugar (Demerara or Turbinado, for a crunchy top — optional)
        amount: to taste
steps:
  - title: Rub the butter into the flour
    text: Mix the flour, sugar and salt, then add the very cold butter. Quickly rub it in with your fingertips (or use a pastry cutter or food processor) until the butter is in pea-sized pieces.
  - title: Add iced water
    text: Add the iced water a little at a time and gently press the mixture together with a scraper or your hands until it forms a dough. Don't knead it — that makes the pastry tough instead of flaky.
  - title: Chill the dough
    text: Divide the dough into a larger and a smaller piece (about 60:40). Shape each into a flat disc, wrap in cling film and refrigerate. Cold dough is easier to roll and less sticky.
    timer: Chill 1 hour or more
  - title: Prepare the apples
    text: Peel and core the apples, cut them into slices about 0.5 cm thick (or small chunks) and toss with the lemon juice.
  - title: Cook the apples
    text: Melt 15 g butter in a frying pan, add the apples and sugar and cook over low heat. The apples will release juice — keep stirring until they soften slightly.
    timer: Cook 5–8 min
  - title: Thicken and cool
    text: Mix the cornflour with a little water, then add it with the cinnamon. Keep stirring until the sauce is thick and coats the apples. Take off the heat and let it cool completely — hot filling melts the butter in the bottom crust.
  - title: Line the pie dish
    text: Roll the larger piece of dough on a lightly floured bench into a circle about 0.3 cm thick and a little bigger than the dish. Lay it in the dish, press it gently against the sides, trim the extra pastry and prick the base all over with a fork.
  - title: Add the filling
    text: Spoon the cooled apple filling into the dish, piling it slightly higher in the middle.
  - title: Cut the strips
    text: Roll out the smaller piece of dough and cut it into even strips about 1.5–2 cm wide with a pastry wheel or knife. You'll need about 8–10 strips.
  - title: Weave the lattice
    text: Lay 4–5 strips across the pie in one direction, evenly spaced. Fold back strips 1, 3 and 5 halfway, lay one new strip across the other way, then fold 1, 3 and 5 back over it. Next fold back strips 2 and 4, lay a second cross strip, and fold 2 and 4 back down. Repeat until the whole top is woven.
  - title: Seal the edge and brush with egg
    text: Press the ends of the strips firmly onto the edge of the bottom crust; you can crimp it into a wavy edge. Brush the top evenly with beaten egg and sprinkle with a little raw sugar for shine and crunch.
  - title: Bake
    text: Preheat the oven to 200°C. Bake at 200°C for 15 minutes to set the pastry, then lower to 180°C and keep baking until the top is deep golden and the filling is bubbling. If the top browns too fast, lay a sheet of foil loosely over it.
    timer: Bake at 200°C 15 min + 180°C 30–35 min
  - title: Cool before slicing
    text: Let the pie cool for at least 1 hour before cutting. The filling needs time to set, or the juices will run all over the plate.
    timer: Cool 1 hour
tips:
  - Keep everything cold — melted butter means pastry that isn't flaky. If the dough gets soft or sticky while rolling or weaving, put it back in the fridge for 10–15 minutes.
  - Make sure the filling is completely cool before it goes into the crust.
  - Best served with a scoop of vanilla ice cream.
