---
title: French Lemon Tart
intro: A crisp sweet pastry shell that stays crunchy, filled with a smooth, rich lemon curd that balances sweet and tart — that's a great lemon tart. This recipe makes one 8-inch (about 20 cm) round tart.
prepTime: about 40 min
waitLabel: Chill
waitTime: pastry 1 hour + setting 3–4 hours
cookLabel: Bake
cookTime: 20–23 min
totalTime: about 6 hours (including chilling)
servings: 1 tart (8-inch tart tin)
ingredients:
  - group: Sweet pastry (pâte sucrée)
    items:
      - name: Unsalted butter (cut into small cubes and kept cold in the fridge)
        amount: 100 g
      - name: Icing sugar (gives the pastry a finer texture)
        amount: 60 g
      - name: Salt
        amount: a pinch
      - name: Egg (room temperature)
        amount: "1"
      - name: Almond meal (adds a nutty flavour and extra crunch)
        amount: 25 g
      - name: Cake flour
        amount: 180 g
  - group: Lemon curd filling
    items:
      - name: Fresh lemon juice (about 3–4 lemons, freshly squeezed is best)
        amount: 100 ml
      - name: Lemon zest (only the yellow part — the white pith is bitter)
        amount: from 2 lemons
      - name: Eggs
        amount: "2"
      - name: Egg yolks
        amount: "2"
      - name: Caster sugar (adjust to taste)
        amount: 100 g
      - name: Unsalted butter (cut into small cubes and softened at room temperature)
        amount: 110 g
steps:
  - title: Mix the dry ingredients
    text: Sift the cake flour, almond meal and icing sugar together, then stir in the salt.
  - title: Rub in the cold butter
    text: Add the cold butter cubes and rub them into the flour with your fingertips or a scraper until it looks like coarse sand. Work quickly and don't overdo it — if the butter melts, the pastry won't be crisp.
  - title: Add the egg
    text: Add the egg and mix just until it comes together into a dough. Don't knead it, or the pastry will turn tough.
  - title: Chill the pastry
    text: Wrap the dough in cling film, flatten it into a disc and refrigerate until firm.
    timer: Chill 1 hour or more
  - title: Roll and line the tin
    text: Lightly flour the bench and rolling pin, then roll the chilled dough into a circle about 0.3 cm thick. Carefully lay it in the 8-inch tart tin and press it into the corners. Roll the rolling pin over the top edge to cut off the extra pastry, then prick the base a few times with a fork so it doesn't puff up.
  - title: Blind bake the shell
    text: Preheat the oven to 180°C. Line the pastry with baking paper and fill it with baking weights (or dried beans or rice). Bake for 15 minutes, then remove the paper and weights and bake until the shell is golden. Let it cool.
    timer: Bake at 180°C 15 min + 5–8 min
  - title: Mix the curd ingredients
    text: In a glass or stainless steel bowl, whisk together the eggs, egg yolks, sugar, lemon juice and lemon zest.
  - title: Cook over a water bath
    text: Set the bowl over a pot of hot water and heat on medium-low, stirring all the time, until the mixture slowly thickens — about 82°C, or thick enough to coat the back of a spoon.
  - title: Stir in the butter
    text: Take it off the heat and let it cool to about 60°C. Add the soft butter a few pieces at a time and blend with a stick blender or whisk until fully combined, smooth and glossy.
  - title: Fill the shell
    text: Strain the lemon curd once, pour it into the cooled tart shell and gently shake the tin to level the top.
  - title: Chill to set
    text: Refrigerate until the lemon filling is completely set.
    timer: Chill 3–4 hours
  - title: Decorate and serve
    text: Just before serving, sprinkle a little lemon zest on top, then slice and enjoy.
tips:
  - Keeping the butter cold is the secret to crisp pastry.
  - "Keep the shell crisp: if you're worried the baked shell will go soft by the next day, brush a thin layer of melted white chocolate inside it once it has cooled."
  - "Adjust the sweetness: for a more tart flavour, cut the sugar to 80 g and add another 10 ml of lemon juice."
---
