---
title: Christmas Gingerbread Men
intro: Classic Christmas gingerbread men with a toasty flavour from brown sugar and honey — crisp, with a warm hint of spice. The dough holds its shape well when cut, so they're perfect for decorating with royal icing. This recipe makes about 18–20 biscuits, depending on the size of your cutter.
prepTime: about 40 min
waitLabel: Chill
waitTime: dough 1 hour + icing drying 1–2 hours
cookLabel: Bake
cookTime: 12–15 min
totalTime: about 3.5 hours (including chilling and drying)
servings: 18–20 biscuits
ingredients:
  - group: Biscuit dough
    items:
      - name: Unsalted butter (softened — a light press with your finger leaves a dent)
        amount: 100 g
      - name: Dark brown sugar or brown sugar (adds caramel flavour and a deeper colour)
        amount: 70 g
      - name: Honey or molasses (molasses tastes most traditional; honey is lighter)
        amount: 45 g
      - name: Beaten egg, room temperature (about half a large egg; a cold egg can make the mixture split)
        amount: 35 g
      - name: Cake flour (sifted)
        amount: 250 g
      - name: Ground cinnamon
        amount: 1.5 tsp
      - name: Ground ginger (use up to 2 tsp if you like it spicier)
        amount: 1.5 tsp
      - name: Ground nutmeg (optional, but it adds more depth)
        amount: 1/4 tsp
      - name: Salt (balances the sweetness)
        amount: a pinch
  - group: Royal icing
    items:
      - name: Meringue powder (safe to eat raw and dries quickly; or use 30 g fresh egg white)
        amount: 10 g
      - name: Warm water (leave out if using fresh egg white)
        amount: 20 ml
      - name: Pure icing sugar (sifted; best without added cornflour)
        amount: 150 g
      - name: Lemon juice (to adjust thickness and remove any egg smell)
        amount: a few drops
steps:
  - title: Cream the butter and sugar
    text: Put the softened butter, brown sugar and salt in a bowl and beat with a whisk until slightly paler and fluffy.
  - title: Add the egg and honey
    text: Add the room-temperature egg in 2 additions, beating well each time. Then add the honey (or molasses) and mix well.
  - title: Add the dry ingredients
    text: Sift the cake flour, cinnamon, ginger and nutmeg into the bowl. Switch to a spatula and use a cutting and pressing motion until no dry flour is left. Don't knead it, or the biscuits will turn tough.
  - title: Roll out and chill
    text: Put the dough between two sheets of baking paper and roll it out to about 4–5 mm thick. Refrigerate it with the paper (or freeze for 20 minutes) until firm. Don't skip this step.
    timer: Chill 1 hour or more
  - title: Cut out and bake
    text: Preheat the oven to 170°C. Take out the firm dough, cut out gingerbread men with a cutter and move them to a baking tray (you can gather the scraps, roll them out, chill and cut again). Bake on the middle rack until the edges are slightly darker.
    timer: Bake at 170°C 12–15 min
  - title: Cool completely
    text: It's normal for the centres to be a little soft straight out of the oven. Move them to a cooling rack and let them cool completely — they'll turn crisp, and the icing won't melt later.
  - title: Make the icing and decorate
    text: Whisk the meringue powder and warm water until foamy, then add the sifted icing sugar and lemon juice a little at a time. When you lift the spatula, the icing should drip down slowly and the trail should stay for about 10 seconds — the right thickness for outlines and faces. Put it in a piping bag, snip a small hole and start decorating. Leave at room temperature to dry and set.
    timer: Dry 1–2 hours
tips:
  - "Keep the shape: if the room is warm and the dough gets soft and sticky while cutting, put it back in the fridge for 10 minutes before you continue. Biscuits that go into the oven cold and firm have the neatest edges and won't spread."
