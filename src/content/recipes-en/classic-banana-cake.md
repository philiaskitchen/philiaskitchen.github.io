---
title: Classic Banana Cake
intro: A classic banana bread — moist, simple to make, and the best way to use up very ripe bananas with spotty black skins. One loaf pan makes a whole loaf.
prepTime: about 15 min
waitLabel: Bake
waitTime: 50–60 min
cookLabel: Cool in the pan
cookTime: 10 min
totalTime: about 1.5 hours
servings: 1 loaf (9×5-inch loaf pan)
ingredients:
  - group: Wet ingredients
    items:
      - name: Very ripe bananas (the riper, the sweeter and more fragrant)
        amount: 3–4 (about 300 g)
      - name: Unsalted butter (melted in the microwave or over hot water)
        amount: 115 g
      - name: Brown sugar or caster sugar (brown sugar gives a darker colour and a caramel flavour)
        amount: 100 g
      - name: Eggs (room temperature)
        amount: "2"
      - name: Vanilla extract
        amount: 1 tsp
  - group: Dry ingredients
    items:
      - name: Plain flour
        amount: 200 g
      - name: Baking powder
        amount: 1 tsp
      - name: Baking soda (bicarb soda)
        amount: 1 tsp
      - name: Salt
        amount: ¼ tsp
  - group: Optional extras
    items:
      - name: Chopped walnuts, pecans or chocolate chips
        amount: ½ cup
steps:
  - title: Preheat and prepare the pan
    text: Preheat the oven to 175°C. Line a 9×5-inch loaf pan with baking paper so the cake is easy to lift out.
  - title: Mash the bananas
    text: Peel the bananas into a large bowl and mash them with a fork. Leave a few small pieces for more texture.
  - title: Mix the wet ingredients
    text: Stir the melted butter into the banana. Then add the brown sugar, the beaten eggs and the vanilla, one at a time, and mix until smooth.
  - title: Add the dry ingredients
    text: Mix the flour, baking powder, baking soda and salt, then sift them into the wet mixture. Fold gently with a silicone spatula, lifting from the bottom, and stop as soon as there is no dry flour left. If you're adding nuts or chocolate chips, fold them in gently at the end.
  - title: Bake
    text: Pour the batter into the pan, smooth the top with a spatula and bake on the middle rack. Check the top at about 40 minutes — if it's browning too fast, lay a sheet of foil loosely over it.
    timer: Bake at 175°C 50–60 min
  - title: Test and cool
    text: Push a skewer or thin chopstick into the centre. If it comes out with no wet batter, it's done. Let it cool in the pan for 10 minutes, then move it to a rack to cool completely before slicing.
    timer: Cool in the pan 10 min
tips:
  - Use the ripest bananas you have — the ones covered in black spots are the sweetest.
  - Don't over-mix once the dry ingredients go in, or the cake will turn tough.
  - If the top is getting too dark around the 40-minute mark, cover it with foil so it doesn't burn.
