---
title: Black Sesame Milk Steamed Buns
intro: The dough is made with fresh milk and black sesame powder, so the buns steam up a soft pale grey and taste of both sesame and milk. Rolling and folding the dough a few extra times gives them a smooth, even surface.
prepTime: about 40 min
waitLabel: Prove
waitTime: 30–40 min
cookLabel: Steam
cookTime: 12–15 min
totalTime: about 1.5 hours
servings: 6–8 buns
ingredients:
  - group: Dough
    items:
      - name: Plain flour
        amount: 300 g
      - name: Fresh milk (cold or room temperature; adjust to how much your flour absorbs)
        amount: about 150–160 g
      - name: Black sesame powder (unsweetened and freshly ground is best)
        amount: 20–25 g
      - name: Caster sugar (adjust to taste)
        amount: 25–30 g
      - name: Instant dried yeast
        amount: 3 g
      - name: Vegetable oil or shortening (makes the dough smoother)
        amount: 5 g
  - group: Filling (optional)
    items:
      - name: Sesame paste
        amount: 30 g
      - name: Icing sugar
        amount: 10 g
steps:
  - title: Dissolve the yeast
    text: Pour the milk into a bowl, add the sugar and yeast, and stir briefly so the yeast dissolves and wakes up.
  - title: Mix until flaky
    text: Add the plain flour and black sesame powder, and stir with chopsticks or a spatula until it looks like flakes with no dry flour left.
  - title: Knead until smooth
    text: Add the oil and start bringing it together by hand. Move it to the bench and keep kneading until your hands, the bowl and the dough are all clean and smooth.
    timer: Knead 8–10 min
  - title: Rest
    text: Cover the dough with cling wrap or a damp cloth and let it rest.
    timer: Rest 5 min
  - title: Roll and fold
    text: Roll the dough into a large, thin rectangle, then fold it in three like a letter. Roll it out and fold it again — repeat 3–4 times until the edges and surface are very smooth with no air bubbles. This is the key to smooth buns.
  - title: Roll up and cut
    text: |-
      Roll the dough into a rectangle about 0.5 cm thick and brush the top with a thin layer of cold water (this helps it stick).
      Roll it up tightly from the long side into a log, then roll it gently under your hands so it is even all the way along.
      Trim both ends with a sharp knife, then cut it into even pieces. Make each cut quick and clean.
  - title: Prove
    text: Place each bun on baking paper in the steamer, leaving space between them. Cover with the lid and leave somewhere warm (about 30–35°C). They are ready when they have grown to about 1.5 times the size, feel light when picked up, and spring back slowly when pressed gently.
    timer: Prove 30–40 min
  - title: Steam and cool
    text: Once the water boils, turn the heat to medium and steam. After turning off the heat, don't open the lid straight away! Open it just a crack and leave for 3 minutes so the temperature drops slowly, then open it fully. This stops the buns shrinking or wrinkling from the sudden cold.
    timer: Steam 12–15 min + rest 3 min
tips:
  - For a richer, oozing centre, mix 30 g sesame paste with 10 g icing sugar and wrap it inside as a filling.
  - The secret to a smooth surface is rolling and folding a few extra times to push all the air out — the buns will come out as smooth as classic milk buns.
  - For more sesame flavour, dry-toast the black sesame powder in a frying pan over low heat for 1–2 minutes and let it cool before adding. Older sesame powder can taste stale.
