---
title: Pastillas de Leche (Milk Candies)
intro: A milk candy from the Philippines. Condensed and evaporated milk plus toasted flour — no oven needed, just one frying pan for rich, milky sweets.
prepTime: about 10 min
cookLabel: Toast flour
cookTime: 5–8 min
totalTime: about 30 min
ingredients:
  - group: Pastillas
    items:
      - name: Plain flour (about 3 tablespoons)
        amount: 45 g
      - name: Milk powder (optional — or use 20 g more flour instead)
        amount: 20 g
      - name: Condensed milk
        amount: 150 ml
      - name: Evaporated milk (lighter and less rich than condensed milk alone)
        amount: 50 ml
      - name: Butter (makes the candies glossy and less sticky)
        amount: 15 g
  - group: To coat
    items:
      - name: Caster sugar
        amount: as needed
steps:
  - title: Toast the flour
    text: Put the flour in a frying pan over very low heat and keep stirring until it turns from pure white to light golden and smells slightly nutty. The flour must be cooked, or the candies will taste of raw flour and stick to your teeth. Tip it out and set aside.
    timer: Toast 5–8 min
  - title: Warm the liquids
    text: In the same pan (no need to wash it), add the condensed milk, evaporated milk and butter. Stir over low heat until the butter has melted and the mixture just starts to bubble.
  - title: Bring it together
    text: Add the toasted flour (sifted is best) in two batches. Keep stirring and pressing with a spatula until the flour has absorbed all the liquid and the mixture is very thick, forms a dough and no longer sticks to the pan. Turn off the heat and tip it onto a plate to cool a little (not cold — just warm is easiest to work with).
  - title: Shape
    text: Rub a little butter on your hands so it doesn't stick. Take a small piece (about 10–15 g), roll it into a log or a ball, then roll it in a plate of caster sugar until evenly coated.
tips:
  - "For a firmer candy, cook it a little longer in the pan to dry it out; for a softer one, take it off the heat as soon as it forms a dough."
  - "For other flavours, add a little cocoa powder or matcha at the \"bring it together\" step."
  - These go really well with strong tea or black coffee.
storage:
  - Wrap each one in baking paper or candy wrappers.
  - They keep for 3–5 days in a cool place, or about 2 weeks in the fridge (they get a little firmer when chilled).
