---
title: Taiwanese Sesame Egg Rolls
intro: Classic crispy Taiwanese sesame egg rolls — this batch makes about 12–15. The secret is plenty of butter and eggs at room temperature, then pressing the batter in a hot egg roll iron so it bakes quickly and dries out completely. That gives you thin, crisp layers that shatter when you bite.
prepTime: about 20 min
waitLabel: Bake in the iron
waitTime: about 1–1.5 min per roll
cookLabel: Cool
cookTime: 10–15 min
totalTime: about 1 hr (incl. cooling)
servings: 12–15 rolls
ingredients:
  - group: Batter
    items:
      - name: Unsalted butter (softened to a soft paste)
        amount: 100 g
      - name: Caster sugar (you can reduce to 60 g)
        amount: 70 g
      - name: Whole eggs (room temperature)
        amount: 3 (about 150 g)
      - name: Cake flour (sifted)
        amount: 100 g
      - name: Toasted black sesame seeds (toast lightly in a dry pan for more flavour)
        amount: 15–20 g
      - name: Salt
        amount: 1/4 tsp
      - name: Vanilla extract (optional, hides any eggy smell)
        amount: 1/2 tsp
  - group: Tools
    items:
      - name: Egg roll iron (stovetop or electric)
        amount: ""
      - name: Steel rod, metal chopstick or thick wooden dowel (about 1.2–1.5 cm wide)
        amount: ""
      - name: Heatproof gloves
        amount: ""
      - name: Cooling rack
        amount: ""
steps:
  - title: Cream the butter and sugar
    text: Put the softened butter in a mixing bowl with the sugar and salt. Beat with an electric mixer (or a whisk) until pale and slightly fluffy. The butter should be a soft paste, not melted.
  - title: Add the eggs bit by bit
    text: Beat the room-temperature eggs, then add them to the butter in 3–4 small batches. Mix well until each batch is fully absorbed before adding the next, so the mixture doesn't split. Add the vanilla now if using.
  - title: Sift in the flour and add the sesame
    text: Sift in the cake flour in two batches and fold it in with a silicone spatula, cutting and turning, until smooth with no dry bits. Don't over-mix. Finally stir in the toasted sesame seeds.
  - title: Heat the iron and add the batter
    text: Put the egg roll iron on the stove and heat both sides well over medium-low heat (a drop of water should sizzle away straight away). Spoon about 1 tablespoon (20–25 g) of batter into the centre.
  - title: Close and bake
    text: Close the iron and press it together firmly and evenly, so the batter spreads to an even thickness. Turn the heat to low and bake each side for about 30–45 seconds. You can open it a crack to check — once both sides are golden, take it off the heat.
    timer: 30–45 sec each side
  - title: Roll while hot, then cool
    text: Open the iron and quickly catch the edge with the rod or chopstick and roll it up — you need to finish within 5 seconds of taking it off the heat. Press the seam for 3 seconds to set, slide out the rod, and let the roll cool completely on a rack.
tips:
  - "Stop the batter splitting: the eggs and butter must both be at room temperature. Cold eggs straight from the fridge make the creamed butter go lumpy and the batter uneven."
  - "Keep the thickness even: press the iron with even pressure. Too thick and the rolls turn hard instead of crisp once cool; too thin and they break when you roll them."
  - "Cool them on a rack: freshly rolled egg rolls are still soft, so let air flow underneath. If you lay them flat on a plate, the steam underneath makes them soft again."
storage:
  - Once completely cool (about 10–15 min), put them straight into a sealed bag or airtight jar with a food-safe desiccant pack. They stay crisp for 2–3 weeks.
