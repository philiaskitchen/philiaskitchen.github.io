---
title: Taiwanese Mini Brown Sugar Fa Gao
intro: Fragrant, soft and bouncy mini brown sugar steamed cakes. Make them in egg tart tins or small ceramic cups — one bite each, and they look so cute. This batch makes about 6–8. They rise mainly with yeast, with just a little baking powder (or none at all). Steam them over high heat from the moment the water boils, and the tops split open beautifully.
prepTime: about 15 min
waitLabel: Rise
waitTime: 40–50 min
cookLabel: Steam
cookTime: 20 min + 3 min rest
totalTime: about 1 hr 20 min
servings: 6–8 cakes
ingredients:
  - group: Batter
    items:
      - name: Plain flour (all-purpose)
        amount: 150 g
      - name: Dark brown sugar (up to 70 g if you like it sweeter)
        amount: 60 g
      - name: Warm water (about 35–40°C, helps the yeast)
        amount: 110 g
      - name: Instant yeast
        amount: 3 g
      - name: Aluminium-free baking powder (optional; a little gives a prettier split top)
        amount: 2 g
      - name: Vegetable oil (keeps the cakes moist)
        amount: 10 g
  - group: Topping
    items:
      - name: Dried red dates (jujubes) or goji berries (optional)
        amount: as needed
steps:
  - title: Dissolve the sugar and wake up the yeast
    text: Stir the brown sugar into the warm water until it fully dissolves. Check that it has cooled to warm but not hot (about 35°C), then gently stir in the yeast. Leave for 5 minutes so the yeast becomes active.
    timer: Rest 5 min
  - title: Mix the batter
    text: Sift the flour (and baking powder, if using) into a large bowl. Pour in the brown sugar yeast water and the oil a little at a time, mixing well with a whisk or spatula until you have a thick, smooth batter with no lumps.
  - title: First rise
    text: Cover the batter with cling wrap or a damp cloth and leave it somewhere warm. The time depends on your room temperature. It's ready when the surface is full of tiny bubbles and it has grown to about 1.5–2 times its size.
    timer: Rise 40–50 min
  - title: Fill the moulds and decorate
    text: Bring the water in your steamer to the boil. Brush the inside of the mini moulds (silicone moulds, egg tart tins or small ceramic cups) with a thin layer of oil. Stir the risen batter a few times to knock out the air, then spoon it into the moulds until 80–90% full. If you like, put a slice of dried red date or a few goji berries on top.
  - title: Steam over high heat
    text: Once the water is boiling, put the moulds in the steamer, close the lid tightly and steam over high heat for 20 minutes. Don't open the lid at all. Turn off the heat and wait 3 minutes before opening the lid — your little brown sugar fa gao are ready.
    timer: Steam 20 min + rest 3 min
tips:
  - "For a nice split top: only put the cakes in after the water is boiling, and keep the heat high with lots of steam the whole time. The sudden heat makes the batter rise fast and split open from the centre — a lucky sign, since “fa” also means “to prosper”."
  - "Choosing the sugar: use a fragrant traditional dark brown sugar (Taiwanese black sugar if you can find it). It gives the most authentic colour and caramel flavour."
