---
title: Classic Canelés de Bordeaux
intro: Traditional French technique plus a beeswax coating for the moulds, for canelés with a glossy, caramelised, crisp shell and a moist, honeycomb-like inside.
prepTime: about 40 min
waitLabel: Rest in fridge
waitTime: 24–48 hours
cookLabel: Bake
cookTime: about 60–70 min
totalTime: about 2 days (incl. resting)
servings: about 8–10 canelés (5.5 cm copper moulds)
ingredients:
  - group: Batter
    items:
      - name: Full-cream milk (richer flavour)
        amount: 500 ml
      - name: Unsalted butter (heated with the milk)
        amount: 50 g
      - name: Vanilla bean (or 1 teaspoon vanilla paste)
        amount: 1 pod
      - name: Caster sugar or icing sugar (key to caramelising — don't cut it much)
        amount: 200 g
      - name: Cake flour (sifted)
        amount: 100 g
      - name: Whole eggs (room temperature)
        amount: "2"
      - name: Egg yolks (room temperature)
        amount: "2"
      - name: Dark rum (for aroma and depth)
        amount: 40 ml
      - name: Salt (for flavour)
        amount: 1/4 teaspoon
  - group: Mould coating
    items:
      - name: Food-grade beeswax (1:1 with butter, lowers the melting point and hardness)
        amount: 25 g
      - name: Unsalted butter
        amount: 25 g
steps:
  - title: Heat the vanilla milk
    text: Put the milk, butter, vanilla seeds and pod in a small saucepan. Heat over medium heat until just simmering (the butter has melted and small bubbles appear at the edge), then take it off the heat and let it cool until warm (about 50–60°C).
  - title: Mix eggs and flour
    text: In a large bowl, gently stir together the whole eggs, yolks, sugar and salt — don't whip, so you don't add too much air. Sift in the cake flour and gently stir from the centre outwards until there are no lumps.
  - title: Add the milk and rum
    text: Take out the vanilla pod. Slowly pour the warm milk into the egg mixture in 3–4 batches, stirring gently as you pour, then mix in the dark rum.
  - title: Strain and rest
    text: Strain the batter 1–2 times to remove lumps and foam. Press cling wrap directly onto the surface of the batter and refrigerate so the flour fully hydrates and the flavours come together.
    timer: Rest 24–48 hours
  - title: Make the beeswax butter
    text: Put 25 g beeswax and 25 g unsalted butter in a heatproof container and melt completely over hot water (about 65°C), stirring well.
  - title: Heat the moulds
    text: Put the clean copper moulds in a 100–120°C oven to heat up. The moulds must be hot so the wax layer isn't too thick.
    timer: Preheat 5–10 min
  - title: Coat and turn out
    text: Take out the hot moulds. Fill the first mould with the melted beeswax butter, quickly pour it into the second mould while turning the first so the inside is evenly coated. Turn the moulds upside down straight away on a wire rack over a tray lined with baking paper, so the extra wax drains out and leaves a very thin layer.
  - title: Set the coating
    text: Turn the coated moulds the right way up and put them in the freezer or fridge so the wax sets completely.
    timer: Chill 10–15 min
  - title: Warm the batter and fill
    text: Preheat the oven to 220°C. Take the rested batter out of the fridge, let it come to room temperature and stir gently with a spatula. Pour it into the moulds until 80% full (leave room to rise).
    timer: Warm up 30 min
  - title: Bake in two stages
    text: "Stage one, high heat to set: bake at 220°C until the tops and sides set quickly and start to brown. Stage two, slow cooking: lower the oven to 180°C and keep baking until they are a deep caramel brown all over."
    timer: 220°C bake 15–20 min + 180°C bake 45–50 min
  - title: Turn out while hot and cool
    text: Turn them out straight away while hot (the beeswax is liquid when hot, so a light tap slides them out). Cool on a wire rack at room temperature until the shell is completely hard and crisp, then enjoy.
    timer: Cool 45–60 min
tips:
  - "To stop the batter rising out of the moulds (the \"muffin top\"): never whip the batter, rest it for the full 24–48 hours, and let it come fully to room temperature before baking."
  - "Caring for copper moulds: don't wash them with water or detergent. While still hot, wipe out the leftover wax and butter with paper towel, and store them dry."
