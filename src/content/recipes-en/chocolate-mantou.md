---
title: Coconut Chocolate Lava Steamed Buns
intro: The lightly sweet, extra-soft version. A cocoa dough with a little coconut oil, filled with chocolate chips that melt when you bite in.
prepTime: 30 min
waitLabel: Prove
waitTime: 40–50 min
cookLabel: Steam
cookTime: 12–15 min
totalTime: about 1.5 hours
servings: 6–8 buns
ingredients:
  - group: Dough
    items:
      - name: Plain flour
        amount: 200 g
      - name: Cake flour
        amount: 80 g
      - name: Cadbury unsweetened cocoa powder
        amount: 20 g
      - name: Caster sugar
        amount: 40 g
      - name: Instant yeast
        amount: 3 g
      - name: Milk (or cold water)
        amount: 150 ml
      - name: Coconut oil (melted until clear)
        amount: 5 g
  - group: Filling
    items:
      - name: Baking chocolate chips, or a chocolate block cut into small pieces
        amount: as needed
steps:
  - title: Mix and knead
    text: Put both flours, the cocoa powder, sugar and yeast in a bowl. Slowly pour in the milk and stir until it looks like flakes. Add the melted coconut oil and knead by hand until the dough is smooth and no longer sticky.
    timer: Knead 7–10 min
  - title: Rest and knock back
    text: Cover the dough with cling wrap or a damp cloth and rest at room temperature for 5 minutes. Roll it out on the bench into a large rectangle to push out the air bubbles, fold it in three, then roll it out once more.
    timer: Rest 5 min
  - title: Fill and cut
    text: |-
      Choose one of these two:
      [Lava buns] For a melting centre: divide the dough into 6–8 pieces and roll each into a circle that is thicker in the middle and thinner at the edge. Put chocolate chips in the centre and pinch the edges together firmly, like a bao. Place seam-side down on baking paper.
      [Chocolate rolls] For chocolate all the way through: scatter the chocolate chips evenly over the large rectangle and press them in lightly. Roll it up tightly into a log, trim the uneven ends, and cut into 6–8 pieces. Place each on baking paper.
  - title: Prove
    text: Space the buns evenly in the steamer, cover with the lid and leave to prove. They are ready when they are 1.5 times the size and spring back slowly when pressed lightly.
    timer: Prove 40–50 min
  - title: Steam and rest
    text: Start with cold water over medium-high heat, and start timing once the water boils. When the time is up, turn off the heat straight away — don't lift the lid yet. Leave for 3–5 minutes, open the lid just a crack to let the steam out, then open it fully.
    timer: Steam 12–15 min + rest 3–5 min
tips:
  - The secret to smooth, unwrinkled buns — never lift the lid as soon as you turn off the heat. Wait 3–5 minutes, then open it just a crack to let the steam out first.
storage:
  - "Cool completely: put the buns on a wire rack as soon as they come out, so steam doesn't make the bottoms soggy."
  - "Freezing: once cool, seal them in a zip-lock bag with the air pressed out and freeze straight away (keeps 3–4 weeks). Don't keep them in the fridge — it makes them dry and hard."
  - "Reheating: no need to thaw. Steam for 10–15 minutes and they are as soft as freshly made. In the microwave, spray the tops with a little water and heat for 40–60 seconds."
